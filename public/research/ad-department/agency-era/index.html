The strategy game of energy, compute and judgment

Agency Era

You are one person on a ridge. The swarm in the valley is as large as the compute you can power. Launch the rocket before quarter 16 ends.

  1. Energy runs compute. Compute runs agents. Whichever is short is your bottleneck.
  2. You get three orders a quarter. Build, redirect a wing of the swarm, or review its work.
  3. Agents drift. The bigger the swarm, the faster it loses the brief. Only you can reset it.
1 player · unlimited agents · about 10 minutes

    How to play

    Goal
    Bring the launch to 100% before quarter 16 ends.
    The chain
    Each unit of energy powers one unit of compute. Each powered unit runs an agent. Racks without power sit dark.
    Orders
    You get three a quarter. Building, redirecting a wing and reviewing the work each cost one. Adopting research is free.
    Wings
    The swarm flies in three wings. Ventures earn capital, research earns points for upgrades, launch work wins the game.
    Direction
    Output is agents times direction. Direction drifts down every quarter, faster with a bigger swarm. A review resets it.
    Keys
    S solar · D data hall · N reactor · R review · E end quarter
