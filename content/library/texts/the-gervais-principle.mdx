## What It Is

The Gervais Principle is Venkatesh Rao's essay series on Ribbonfarm that uses The Office to explain how companies actually behave. The core frame is a three-layer hierarchy, then a broader language for status, incentives, and the social theater that shapes life inside firms.

## When To Use It

Use it when thinking about org design, management pathologies, middle-layer behavior, career strategy inside companies, or how status games distort what people say versus what they mean.
