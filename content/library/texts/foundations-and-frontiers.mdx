## What It Is

Foundations & Frontiers is Contrary Research's long-form series on major deep-tech domains, from power and chips to robotics, biology, materials, and space. It is less a single thesis piece than a durable map of adjacent sectors that matter when AI growth runs into physical and industrial constraints.

## When To Use It

Use it when you need a clean primer on energy, compute, robotics, manufacturing, or other industrial systems that sit underneath the next wave of AI deployment.
