## What It Is

The Techno-Industrial Policy Playbook is a multi-author volume from Foundation for American Innovation, American Compass, Institute for Progress, and the NAIA Foundation. It turns the techno-industrial thesis into concrete policy proposals across permitting, nuclear finance, shipbuilding, export controls, minerals, federal R&D, and special compute zones.

## When To Use It

Use it when thinking about US industrial policy, defense capacity, compute infrastructure, energy finance, or the policy layer that shapes where AI and manufacturing capability can actually get built.
