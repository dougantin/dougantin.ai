## What It Is

The Age of the Solopreneur is a Stripe Economics research post on how nonemployer businesses are growing, why solo founders are showing up at higher revenue thresholds, and how AI appears to be filling capability gaps that used to force early hiring.

## When To Use It

Use it when writing about AI and labor, one-person companies, internet business formation, or the claim that agency, not raw access to intelligence, is becoming the scarcer lever.
