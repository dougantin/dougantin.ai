## What It Is

Fish Audio is a voice AI platform for expressive text-to-speech, speech-to-text, voice cloning, and real-time streaming. It combines a web product, APIs, SDKs, and agent-facing integrations for teams building audio into products or content systems.

## When To Use It

Use it when evaluating the audio layer for AI-native products, support bots, narration workflows, or agent systems that need voice output, transcription, or fast iteration on cost and quality.
