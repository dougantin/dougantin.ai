## What It Is

Adapt is designed as an integrated company coworker inside Slack and the web, using shared knowledge and connected systems to answer questions, prepare materials, and help teams act on operational context.

## When To Use It

Use it when evaluating Slack-centric company AI products that aim to serve many teams at once without making AI access feel like a separate expert tool.
