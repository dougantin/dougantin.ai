## What It Is

Symbolic.ai is built for newsroom and communications workflows where teams need AI help across research, drafting, formatting, fact-checking, and house-style consistency.

## When To Use It

Use it when the goal is repeatable, collaborative editorial production rather than ad hoc solo prompting, especially in journalism, corporate communications, or other high-accountability publishing contexts.
