## What It Is

Replit Design is an AI creative environment for generating and refining product, website, and marketing design inside Replit. It is positioned around continuous iteration, reusable design systems, and staying close to the code and publishing stack.

## When To Use It

Use it when comparing tools that collapse design and implementation into one workflow, especially for founders, PMs, or operators who want to move from idea to shippable interface without a heavier design handoff.
