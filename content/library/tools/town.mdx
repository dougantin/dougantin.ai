## What It Is

Town positions itself as a persistent AI assistant that accumulates context from how a person works, then helps with drafting, scheduling, follow-ups, briefings, and multi-step coordination across common work tools.

## When To Use It

Use it as a reference point when tracking the personal AI assistant category, especially products that aim to act like a real operator layer rather than a generic chatbot.
