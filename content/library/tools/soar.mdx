## What It Is

Soar is a consumer flight-search and booking product built around low live fares, one-click rebooking, automated check-in, and lightweight trip management after purchase.

## When To Use It

Use it when you want a cleaner travel workflow for finding cheap flights, booking quickly, and handling the operational steps around a trip without juggling multiple tools.
