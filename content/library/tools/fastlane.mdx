## What It Is

Fastlane is designed around short-form growth output: trend remixing, AI-assisted content generation, scheduling, and performance tracking across the main vertical-video platforms.

## When To Use It

Use it when a team needs volume short-form distribution for product or brand growth and cares more about throughput and iteration than handcrafted premium editing.
